Process

Simple by design

No specialist equipment, no app, no account needed — just a marked spot, a camera, and an email address.

1

Set up a photopoint station

Choose a spot in your landscape and mark the exact camera position — a fence post, painted rock, or notch in a tree. Every future photo comes from the same point.

2

Community members email photos

Anyone visiting takes a photo from the marked position and emails it to your station's unique address. No app or upload portal required.

3

Tupureel builds your timelapse

Photos are aligned, sorted, and assembled into a timelapse automatically. Watch seasons change, plantings establish, and wildlife return — one frame at a time.

About

Tupureel

Tupureel began as a small experiment: could the humble, shareable photograph become a tool for community science and landscape stewardship? Repeat photography — taking a photo from exactly the same spot, over and over, across months and years — is one of the most powerful and accessible ways to document ecological change.

Tupureel makes that practice easy for any community group, anywhere in Aotearoa New Zealand. No complex equipment, no expertise, no cost. Just a marked spot, a community, and time.

The service is entirely free to use. There is no subscription, no advertising, and no data sold.

Always free
